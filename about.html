Timelace

Plan your day and see, at a glance, when you're free.

Timelace is a free day planner that works in your browser and installs like an app on phones and laptops.

Open Timelace

What it does

  • Plan in plain words. Type “tomorrow 10am gym” or drag on the day chart.
  • Am I free? Type a day or time and it tells you, with the free gaps around it.
  • Schedule shows what's on now and next, with reminders.
  • Fit a task finds free time for something like “report, 2 hours, by Friday”, splitting it if needed.
  • People lets you keep the routines of up to three people and send one merged chart showing when everyone is free.
  • Where did the time go? Log how your hours were really spent.
  • Works offline, in several languages and themes.

Your data stays yours

Your plans are kept on your own device. If you choose to, Timelace can keep your devices in step through a hidden file in your own Google Drive. Timelace has no database of its own and does not collect, sell or share your data. Read the privacy policy.

Light on the planet

Timelace stores nothing on our servers and syncs only when something changes. A sync with no changes is a single tiny check; backups are small text files.
