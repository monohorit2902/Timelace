Timelace

Plan your day and see, at a glance, when you're free.

Timelace is a free day planner that works in your browser and installs like an app on phones and laptops.

Open Timelace

How it works

  1. Add plans. Type or speak them (“tomorrow 10am gym”), drag on the day chart, or upload a calendar file (.ics), timetable or PDF.
  2. Ask. Type “fri 7pm” or “14th” to see what's on, where plans clash and who in your circle is around. “then” chains plans; “every monday” repeats them.
  3. Summarize and fit. See your day at a glance, and say a job, how long and by when to place it where it suits everyone.
  4. Your circle. Save up to 3 people's routines, compare them with yours, and send one merged chart of when everyone is free.
  5. Share, remind, chart. Send your routine as a file, set reminders, or save a day as a picture.

Also in Timelace

  • Schedule shows what's happening now and next, with reminders.
  • Where did the time go? Log how your hours were really spent.
  • Works offline, installs like an app, in several languages and themes.

Your data stays yours

Your plans are kept on your own device. If you choose to, Timelace can keep your devices in step through a hidden file in your own Google Drive. Timelace has no database of its own and does not collect, sell or share your data. Read the privacy policy.

Light on the planet

Timelace stores nothing on our servers and syncs only when something changes. A sync with no changes is a single tiny check; backups are small text files.
